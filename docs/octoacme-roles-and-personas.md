# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Test Engineers

### Role Summary
QA / Test Engineers validate that software meets requirements, catches regressions early, and helps teams deliver trustworthy releases.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and coverage for new features and fixes
- Validate acceptance criteria and readiness for release
- Identify defects, reproduce issues, and track bug severity
- Collaborate with Developers on testability and regression prevention
- Support smoke testing and release quality gates

### Goals
- Reduce risk of defects in production
- Improve confidence in release quality
- Align testing effort with product and technical priorities

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, bug reports, and quality signoff meetings
- Daily standups and release-readiness reviews
- Feedback loops with Product Managers on acceptance expectations

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works closely with Developers to validate behavior, clarify defects, and improve automation coverage.
- Partners with Product Managers to confirm that acceptance criteria are measurable and testable.
- Keeps Project Managers informed of quality risks, release blockers, and deployment confidence.

---

## UX / Product Designers

### Role Summary
UX / Product Designers improve the customer experience by shaping user flows, interactions, and design decisions that support product goals.

### Responsibilities
- Research user needs, pain points, and usability opportunities
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Define interaction patterns and accessibility expectations
- Collaborate on feature prioritization and trade-off decisions
- Validate product decisions through usability testing and feedback

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and valuable user experiences
- Improve product adoption and customer satisfaction
- Reduce rework by aligning design decisions early

### Typical Communication
- Discovery sessions and design reviews
- Product backlog discussions and feature clarifications
- Feedback loops with Developers and QA during implementation and validation

### Interaction with existing roles
- Aligns with Product Managers on customer outcomes, priorities, and success metrics.
- Works with Developers to ensure implementation matches the intended experience and is technically feasible.
- Provides QA with user flows and edge cases that should be validated before release.

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads / Architects set the technical direction, manage key design decisions, and help the team deliver scalable, maintainable solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Guide architecture, technical standards, and integration patterns
- Review critical design decisions and technical trade-offs
- Identify dependencies, platform risks, and delivery constraints
- Support team technical mentoring and planning discussions
- Ensure the solution remains aligned with long-term maintainability goals

### Goals
- Deliver solutions that are scalable, secure, and maintainable
- Reduce avoidable technical debt and integration risk
- Support the team in making sound implementation decisions

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical design sessions
- Dependency planning with PMs and engineering leads
- Escalation paths for major technical risks or blockers

### Interaction with existing roles
- Partners with Developers on design quality, implementation standards, and technical trade-offs.
- Provides Product Managers and Project Managers with insight into dependencies, effort implications, and delivery risk.
- Works with QA to ensure testing covers critical technical failure modes and integration points.

---

## Release / Operations Engineers

### Role Summary
Release / Operations Engineers help teams ship changes safely and maintain service health before, during, and after deployment.

### Responsibilities
- Prepare deployment plans, rollback readiness, and operational checks
- Manage monitoring, alerting, and incident readiness for releases
- Coordinate environment readiness, staging validation, and production handoff
- Support post-deployment verification and issue triage
- Help maintain operational resilience during change windows

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and production disruption
- Improve deployment confidence and observability
- Maintain stable service performance after change rollout

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and deployment checklists
- Incident response coordination and post-deploy verification updates
- Communication with Project Managers and stakeholders on timing and risks

### Interaction with existing roles
- Collaborates with Developers and QA on smoke tests, validation criteria, and rollback planning.
- Provides Project Managers with operational risk updates and release timing guidance.
- Works with Product Managers to align launch windows with business priorities and stakeholder expectations.

---

## Business / Domain Stakeholders

### Role Summary
Business / Domain Stakeholders represent the customer, business, or operational context that defines the value and constraints of the work.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business goals, domain context, and decision framing
- Validate that requirements reflect real use cases and priorities
- Help weigh trade-offs between scope, schedule, and operational impact
- Approve key milestones, changes, and adoption decisions when required
- Provide feedback on business readiness and value realization

### Goals
- Ensure work delivers meaningful business value
- Align delivery with real customer and operational needs
- Support informed decision-making throughout the project lifecycle

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder reviews, roadmap briefings, and milestone approvals
- Feedback on requirements, delivery progress, and adoption readiness
- Coordination through Product Managers and Project Managers

### Interaction with existing roles
- Partner with Product Managers to define outcomes, priorities, and success metrics.
- Support Project Managers with approvals, dependencies, and communication needs.
- Provide context to Developers and QA so the team can validate business value and release readiness.

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- The extended model helps teams clarify accountability, handoffs, and collaboration patterns across product, engineering, quality, delivery, and stakeholder groups.
